# BudgetBasics Cinematic Showcase Design

## Goal

Create a professional 2–3 minute PowerPoint showcase of the completed BudgetBasics website. The deck should borrow the reference video's promotional rhythm—kinetic typography, glow, perspective screenshots, and rapid visual transitions—while remaining recognizably BudgetBasics.

## Visual Direction

- Use the existing forest green, honey, cream, leaf green, and white palette.
- Alternate dark cinematic scenes with bright product scenes.
- Use oversized short headlines, soft radial glows, thin orbital lines, and angled browser frames.
- Keep each slide focused on one promotional statement or one website interaction.
- Preserve readable contrast and avoid placing essential text inside screenshots.

Use 18 scenes totaling approximately 150 seconds:

1. Institutional logos and team reveal.
2. BudgetBasics title reveal.

4. Product promise: clear, judgment-free guidance.
5. Home page hero reveal.
6. Learn Budgeting route and dropdown structure.
7. Needs vs. Wants learning scene.
8. Explore Resources and infographic filtering.
9. Budget calculator setup.
10. Calculator result Morph zoom.
11. Savings goal setup and result.
12. Expense planner entries.
13. Expense planner totals Morph zoom.
14. Search and local chatbot.
15. Feedback and local-only privacy.

17. Closing product promise.
18. Final team/end card.

## Motion and Timing

- Add native PowerPoint Morph to all scene transitions where supported.
- Explicitly match the calculator and planner screenshots across their paired slides for reliable Morph zooms.
- Use fade as the compatibility fallback.
- Advance automatically after 6–12 seconds per slide, targeting 140–170 seconds total.
- Keep the opening credits long enough to read every name.
- Use speaker notes for narration; on-slide copy stays brief.

## Content Rules

- Use screenshots captured from the completed local website.
- Do not claim backend storage, live AI, analytics, Lighthouse results, or network submission.
- Describe the chatbot as rule-based and local.
- Describe calculators as educational estimates, not financial advice.
- Keep verification claims limited to evidence already present in the project.

## Deliverable and Verification

- Produce one final `.pptx` in `documentation/`.
- Validate package integrity, slide count, speaker notes, transition XML, and automatic timings.
- Render every slide and inspect it for clipping, overflow, unreadable text, and poor screenshot crops.
- Retain only the final user-facing deck after verification when cleanup is safe.
